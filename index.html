The Spark

My journey began in 2019 using Azure IoT to solve a very real agricultural problem: tracking livestock to prevent financial loss. That success commercialized into a smart IoT solution and launched my career in tech-driven products.

The Ecosystem

Today, I head the product division for Google Cloud. Across both platforms, I've helped shape a combined $130M business operating across 26 countries by constantly building net-new revenue streams.

The consistent pattern to this growth:

  • Start with a real customer problem
  • Stay obsessively focused on solving it
  • Combine that with a mindset of continuous learning and evolution

That intersection—customer obsession and constant growth—is where I’ve found my passion.
